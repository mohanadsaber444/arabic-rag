Ofuq Digital Solutions - IT Security Policy

Passwords
Passwords must be at least 12 characters long and changed every 90 days. Multi-factor authentication is required for all company accounts.

Devices
Company laptops must use full-disk encryption. Lost or stolen devices must be reported to the IT helpdesk within 24 hours.

Incidents
Security incidents are reported to the security team. The security team responds to critical incidents within 2 hours.

Software
Only software approved by the IT department may be installed. Requests for new software are answered within 5 business days.

Backups
Company data is backed up daily at 02:00 and backups are kept for 30 days.
